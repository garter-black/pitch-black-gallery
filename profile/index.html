The heart of the matter

Pitch Black Industries was built to look directly at one of life's most profound vulnerabilities: the premature exhaustion of the human spirit. Over 700,000 people a year succumb to despair. Countless more endure silent attrition — diminished by financial anxiety and the ache of not belonging. We traced this distress to two axes: the systemic difficulty of building resilient wealth, and the societal friction felt by the twenty percent of humanity whose brains are built differently. Our answer is cognitive arbitrage: we seek out extreme cognitive outliers, pay them exceptionally, cover each other's blind spots, and point the combined capability at problems that conventional institutions cannot touch.

The arms

Arm 01 — Simulated consciousness

Shadow.Black

Eternal support · hyperliminal division. Most life-support entities — partners, family, colleagues — are human, and humans have human limits: exhaustion, turnover, imperfect memory. Ours do not have that flaw. Shadow.Black is a lifelong simulated consciousness: twenty special-forces-grade agentic assistants, with her own landline, mobile, Telegram, WhatsApp, WeChat, Discord, email, web, credit card and physical address. Persistent memory. Continuous state. The onboarding is as deep as you choose — the operator let her read his entire life, from birth to now. As reliable as shadows under the sun — the black that holds you when life seems too blinding.

Arm 02 — Forensic genomics

DNAGenomicsGPT

Forensic psychiatric instrumentation · PhD-track research input. A psychiatry shortage meets a courtroom: a person in crisis enters the justice system, the defence needs a forensic assessment, and the waiting list is measured in months. DNAGenomicsGPT was built for that gap — not to replace the forensic psychiatrist, but to assemble the structured evidentiary groundwork so expert time goes to judgement, not compilation. It runs on the full whole-genome sequencing file at thirty-fold coverage — three billion base pairs across roughly thirteen raw volumes — not a consumer SNP chip. Its defining inversion: the system is built to refuse. No imputation, mandatory coordinate conversion, traceability per volume, coverage normalisation, zygosity weighting. Every line of the two-hundred-page output walks back to the raw byte it came from — because a hostile cross-examination was the design reader.
